How It Works

The Exact Steps, Laid Out For You

This is the real process from real Nigerian digital-product sellers — simplified into steps you can follow today.

01

Step 1 — Create the digital product first

Don't start with ads. Start with something ready to sell: a PDF, guide, or template. Get it ready.

  • Pick a niche that already spends money (relationship, mothers & babies, money/finance, health)
  • Build it in Canva or Google Docs — free tools, works on your phone
  • Price it (₦1,000–₦5,000 for most first products)
  • No product, no ads. Product first — always.
02

Step 2 — Raise ₦30,000 to promote it

You need seed money for ads. The fastest honest way: talking flyers for small online businesses around you.

  • Charge ₦5,000 – ₦10,000 per talking flyer
  • 5 clients can give you ₦30,000 or more
  • Walk into shops, send WhatsApp, post your work, bill your friends & family
  • Don't hide. Be shameless. "You can't be broke and shy at the same time."
03

Step 3 — Run ads with that ₦30K

Take the money and promote your digital product with Facebook Ads — Advantage+ audience, wide targeting.

  • Best niches that sell: love / relationship / marriage, mothers & nursing mothers
  • Don't make the ad explicit — Meta will reject it
  • Sell the result: better relationship, better marriage, help for new mothers
  • Use video creatives (your talking-flyer skills transfer here)
04

Step 4 — Rinse & repeat

One sale is not the goal. It's a system, not a lottery ticket.

  • Sell → get more talking-flyer clients → run more ads → improve the product → repeat
  • Consistency first. Scaling later.
  • This is how you move from ₦5K daily to bigger numbers
