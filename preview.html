The ₦5K–₦10K Daily Promise

An honest chapter about what this system is, what it isn't, and why ₦5,000–₦10,000 a day is a realistic goal — not hype.

Let's start with honesty, because that's the whole point of this book.

I am not going to promise you ₦100,000 or ₦200,000 every single day. Anyone who promises that on the internet is selling you dreams, not a system.

What I am showing you is a simple process — the same process real Nigerian digital-product sellers are using right now. If you put in the work, ₦5,000 – ₦10,000 daily is possible. And once you have that consistency, you scale.

Let's do the math, because numbers don't lie:

₦5,000/day × 30 days = ₦150,000/month
₦10,000/day × 30 days = ₦300,000/month

That's ₦150K – ₦300K monthly — more than many 9–5 salaries in Nigeria — from a business you run on your phone.

Why Digital Products?

Digital products are simply things you create once and sell many times — a PDF guide, a template, an ebook, a checklist. There's no inventory to store, no shipping to pay for, and delivery is instant. Every sale is almost pure profit.

People all over Africa are buying them every single day: relationship guides, pregnancy and baby care books, recipe books, finance templates, business checklists, church and event materials — the list is endless.

The best part? You don't need to be an expert or an influencer. You need a product people want, a way to get it in front of them, and a system to do it repeatedly.

💡 Remember this line: "You can't be broke and shy at the same time." This book will push you to be shameless in the right way — talking to businesses, pitching your product, showing up until you get paid.
