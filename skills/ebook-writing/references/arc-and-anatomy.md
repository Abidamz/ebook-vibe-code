# The 10-beat arc & chapter anatomy

The arc is the product. Readers pay for the feeling that a chaotic domain has an order.
Map ANY topic onto these beats; merge beats only when the topic truly lacks one.

## The arc (with the worked example → generic label)

| # | Beat | Generic purpose | Example (digital products) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Orientation | Promise + honesty contract: what this IS / is NOT, who it's for, who should quit now | "What this book IS / is NOT" |
| 2 | 101 & landscape | Define the field; list the hottest entry points/niches; kill intimidation | Digital Products 101 + niches |
| 3 | Step 1 — Create | The core craft as a numbered process (5–8 steps) | 7-step product creation |
| 4 | Leverage shortcut | A legitimate faster path for the impatient, with ethics guardrails | Resell/PLR responsibly |
| 5 | Step 2 — Seed money | How to fund/start with almost nothing; include word-for-word scripts | Talking flyers → ₦30,000 |
| 6 | Step 3 — Distribution | Getting seen/paid: channel setup, creatives, first sales | Facebook Ads that sell |
| 7 | Step 4 — Systemize & scale | Turn wins into a repeatable loop; the master checklist | 10K-daily system checklist |
| 8 | Pro bonus | One advanced, differentiating tactic competitors' books lack | Country-targeting trick |
| 9 | 30-day plan | The whole book compressed into a dated action grid | 30-Day Action Plan |
| 10 | Worksheets & appendix | Fill-in pages, daily tracker, all sources grouped by chapter | Worksheets + source links |

Beats 3–7 are the spine: **create → leverage → fund → distribute → scale.** If your topic
has no money-raising beat (e.g. a skill like drawing), beat 5 becomes "equip yourself for
almost nothing" — same function: remove the resource excuse.

## Chapter anatomy (every chapter, no exceptions)

1. **Opener** — `CHAPTER N` kicker, serif title, one-line promise ("What this chapter
   gives you: …"), 2–4 bullets of outcomes.
2. **Sections** — 2–4 `h2` heads; under each, paragraphs ≤4 lines + ✓ bullet lists.
   One idea per section. Numbers and named examples over adjectives.
3. **Myth-bust box** — ✗ list of the 2–4 wrong beliefs this chapter kills
   ("What this is NOT", "Mistakes that keep you broke").
4. **DO-THIS-NOW box** — 1–3 concrete actions, written as commands with a finish line
   ("Draft your 10-line contents today, not tomorrow.").
5. **Sources & References** — the links/threads this chapter builds on (clickable).

Target 3–4 pages per chapter. A chapter that runs 6+ pages must become two chapters.

## Front matter (before chapter 1)

- Cover: title, accent line, subtitle, price line, terms, author.
- Contents: numbered chapter list.
- Orientation page(s): the honesty contract — IS / IS-NOT lists, audience list,
  "put this book down now if…" line, how to use the book (tasks matter more than reading).

## Back matter

- 30-day plan grid (beat 9).
- Worksheets + daily tracker (beat 10).
- Appendix: every source link grouped by chapter (mirrors the per-chapter boxes).
- Final page: author note + where to get help/community + copyright & disclaimer.

## Free-sample split

Sample PDF = cover-lite + chapter 1 + a closing CTA page ("This was Chapter 1 of N" +
what the full book unlocks + checkout link). Chapter 1 must teach something genuinely
useful alone — a sample that withholds all value converts nobody.
