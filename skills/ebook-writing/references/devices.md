# Paying-for-itself devices

These are the pages readers screenshot and share. They convert "information" into "a tool".

## DO-THIS-NOW task box (every chapter)

- 1–3 commands, each with a finish line and a size ("10 lines", "one evening", "3 names").
- Written as imperatives; optionally numbered.
- Ends with a why-it-matters sentence ("Visualize it — it matters.").
- Rendered as a tinted box (amber on parchment) so it is findable by thumbing pages.

## Myth-bust list (every chapter)

- 2–4 ✗ items: the wrong belief, stated in the reader's own inner voice.
- Pair each with a one-line correction in the following paragraph.

## Checklists (✓ bullets)

- For any process ≥4 steps, ship it twice: once as prose steps, once as a bare ✓ list
  the reader can tick. The bare list is what gets printed and taped to a wall.

## Scripts & templates (word-for-word)

- Wherever the reader must talk/write to someone (pitch, DM, ad copy, refund policy),
  give literal fill-in-the-blank text in a monospace-ish block. This is the highest-value
  device in the book; readers cannot invent language under pressure.

## 30-day plan grid (back matter)

- Weeks 1–4 as blocks; each day one line: `Day N — one action`.
- Actions must reference chapters ("Day 6 — run the Chapter 3 checklist on your idea").
- Include 2 rest/review days per week labelled as such (honesty doctrine: pace is real).

## Daily tracker (worksheet)

- Table: date | action done (✓) | money in | money out | one lesson.
- 30 rows. Empty grids are content; do not describe them, ship them.

## Worksheets (fill-in pages)

- Prompts from the task boxes, expanded: 6–10 ruled prompts per worksheet page.
- Header each with the chapter it belongs to so readers can photocopy legally-ish and
  still find their way back.

## References box + appendix

- Per chapter: `Sources & References` — label + clickable URL lines (cobalt).
- Appendix: same links grouped `Chapter N:` so the book is a curated library, which
  justifies price on its own ("every source, vetted and ordered").

## Cover & contents conventions

- Cover: accent kicker, serif title (one line may switch to sans accent colour),
  subtitle promise, price line + terms, author byline.
- Contents: numbered, no page numbers if the builder can't guarantee them — a clean
  numbered list beats wrong numbers.

## Sample-chapter CTA page

- "This was Chapter 1 of N." → "Get the complete book and unlock:" + ✓ list of the
  remaining beats → price line (anchor struck) → terms → CTA callout with checkout link.
- The ✓ list here is your sales page's "what's inside" section — write it once, reuse.
