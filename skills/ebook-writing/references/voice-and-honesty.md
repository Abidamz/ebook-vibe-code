# Voice, honesty & ethics

## Voice rules (the no-hype doctrine)

- Second person, present tense, short sentences. Average ≤ 15 words/sentence.
- Concrete over impressive: "₦30,000", "day 12", "10 lines", not "significant capital".
- Name the work in the same breath as the reward: "This takes an evening. Do it anyway."
- Contractions and plain words. If a sentence sounds like a brochure, rewrite it.
- One metaphor per chapter, maximum. Prefer verbs to figures of speech.
- Readability target ≈ grade 8. Read every task box aloud; if you stumble, simplify.

## Claims discipline

| Never | Instead |
|---|---|
| "guaranteed income" | "a system that targets …" |
| "passive from day one" | "repeatable once it works" |
| "secret trick" | "the step most people skip" |
| "you will make X" | "sellers who do this typically work toward X" |
| "no effort" | "no tech skills — but real work" |

- Every outcome claim is paired with the action that earns it, in the same paragraph.
- Include at least one "who this is NOT for" statement per book, and one per ad/sample.
- Price honesty: show compare-at only if it was a real previous price.

## Attribution & sourcing (mined-content rules)

Most playbook ebooks are expanded from public threads, talks, or notes. That is
legitimate ONLY with transformation + citation:

1. **Cite per chapter**: every thread/post/talk a chapter builds on goes in that
   chapter's references box (author handle + URL) and in the appendix.
2. **Transform, don't transcribe**: restructure into steps, add worksheets, examples,
   scripts, checklists, and the honesty framing. Verbatim lifts beyond short quotes need
   permission.
3. **Add original value** equal to or greater than the source: the 30-day plan, the
   tracker, the myth-busts and task boxes count; mere rewording does not.
4. **Credit in front matter** too: "inspired by free public educational threads by …".
5. If the source has a licence or the platform's terms restrict commercial reuse,
   get written permission BEFORE selling. When in doubt, paraphrase + cite + link.

## Mandatory disclaimer block (adapt per market/topic)

> This book is educational. It describes one practitioner's system, not a promise of
> earnings. Results depend on your work, market and timing. Nothing here is financial,
> legal or professional advice for your situation. Regulated topics (money, health, law):
> consult a licensed professional.

Place it on the copyright page and repeat the one-line version near the price in any
marketing artifact.

## Tone calibration by beat

- Orientation: firm, warm, blunt.
- Steps: imperative, sequenced, anticipates failure ("when this flops — it will — do X").
- Myth-bust: sharp but never mocking the reader; mock the myth.
- Tasks: coach voice, finish lines, no vagueness ("write 10 lines" not "start outlining").
- Plan/worksheets: neutral, scannable, zero prose.
